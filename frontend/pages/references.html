AI prompts used during development

This project was developed with the assistance of Claude (Anthropic). In accordance with the course's academic integrity policy, the prompts given to the AI during Phase 2 development are logged below verbatim. The AI generated the initial front-end code (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript) from the Phase 1 planning & design document; the developer reviewed, tested, adjusted, and deployed the result.

Prompt 1

Uploaded the "Web Project Assignment Phase 2: Front-end implementation" specification, followed by: "do you understand the assignment and the deliverables well enough now?"

Prompt 2

"what do you need from me to do my deliverables / can I get Cowork to do most of it for me"

Prompt 3

Uploaded the Phase 1 Planning & Design document (ArtConnect) and provided: developer email, GitHub username, "look those up yourself" (for public-domain image sourcing), and "Do whichever is easiest and best for you to do" (for the multimedia element). The AI then generated the complete front-end: index.html, gallery.html, events.html, submit.html, faq.html, references.html, style.css, script.js, and SVG assets, implementing the color palette, typography, and page structure specified in the Phase 1 document.

Note: The Phase 1 planning document itself was also prepared with AI assistance, as documented in that phase's submission.
