Anyone — from first-time creators to established professionals. ArtConnect was built specifically so artists without technical skills or a marketing budget can have a professional online presence. Use the Submit Art page to send your piece to the curation team; there is no application fee.

No. Displaying artwork in the gallery and listing events is free for artists. If a piece sells through a connection made on the platform, the sale is handled directly between artist and buyer — ArtConnect does not process payments or take a commission.

You set your own price when you submit. If a piece is for display only, enter a price of 0 and it will be labeled "Not for Sale" in the gallery. You can request a price change at any time by emailing the curation team.

Community members propose exhibitions, workshops, and open studios, and the ArtConnect team publishes events that are open to the public and art-focused. Each listing includes the date, venue, description, and any fees, with full details revealed on the event card.

Artists always retain full copyright to their work. Submitting a piece only grants ArtConnect permission to display it in the gallery and in event promotion. The historical masterworks currently on display are in the public domain, served from Wikimedia Commons, and credited on the References page.
